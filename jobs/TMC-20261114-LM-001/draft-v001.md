## A2. Side-by-side quotation worksheet

**How to use it**

1. Enter each quotation exactly as written.
2. Add every excluded cost to that quotation's total.
3. Compare the payable totals, not the headline prices.
4. For each difference, ask: does it serve our purpose, save families a later cost, reduce teacher workload or reduce a risk we would otherwise carry?
5. If the options deliver equivalent value, the lower price is a sound choice.

## A3. Seven booking questions to settle before parents pay

1. What is the total payable cost per student, including taxes?
2. What is not included?
3. When are payments due, and in how many instalments?
4. What is the minimum group size, and what happens to the price if fewer students join?
5. Under what conditions can the price change after booking?
6. What are the cancellation charges, and from which date do they apply?
7. What is refunded, when and how, if a student withdraws or the trip is cancelled?

Terms differ by programme and provider. Get them in writing for your trip.
