# Case study template — TEMPLATE ONLY, NO REAL CLIENT DATA

Every bracketed field below is a required input, not a placeholder to be filled with an invented value. This structure follows `skills/tmc-case-study/SKILL.md`'s five-part framework exactly.

---

**Headline:** [School name, or "A [type of school]" if anonymised] — [one-line summary of the outcome, once known]

## 1. Situation

[What kind of school, what kind of trip, what they were trying to achieve. Factual, no embellishment.]

## 2. Complication

[What made the usual approach insufficient — a specific constraint, a past bad experience, a logistical problem, a safeguarding concern, whatever it actually was.]

## 3. Question / challenge

[The specific question the school needed answered before they could proceed with confidence.]

## 4. Answer / TMC intervention

[What TMC actually did or changed — specific enough that another school could picture it. Not a general claim like "we provided excellent service."]

## 5. Impact / outcome

[What actually happened afterward, in the school's own account. If there's a number here — attendance, a satisfaction figure, a repeat-booking decision — name its source. If there's a quote, it must be exact and attributed with permission.]

## What another school can learn

[One or two sentences connecting this specific outcome to a general lesson — without overstating it into a universal guarantee.]

## Repurposing note

Once populated with real, permissioned facts, this single case study can become a full blog post, a LinkedIn post, a marketing carousel, a newsletter feature, or a short proof card — per `skills/tmc-case-study/SKILL.md`'s output variants. Each repurposed version still needs its own review; approving the source case study does not auto-approve every adaptation.
