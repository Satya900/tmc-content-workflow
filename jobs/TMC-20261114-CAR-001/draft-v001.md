## A1. LinkedIn document post (school buyers), 7 panels, 1080 × 1350

| Panel | Display text (Staatliches) | Body (DM Sans) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Cover | DAY TRIP, DOMESTIC TOUR OR INTERNATIONAL PROGRAMME? | Which fits your school's purpose and budget |

| 3 | DAY TRIP | **Age:** suits every grade, including the youngest. **Experience:** one place, one theme, explored properly. **Time:** one school day, no nights away. **Family budget:** the lowest of the three. **Readiness:** no time away from home needed. |
| 4 | DOMESTIC TOUR | **Age:** [VERIFY: TMC's usual grade range]. **Experience:** several connected places over a few days. **Time:** overnight stays, [VERIFY: typical number of nights]. **Family budget:** moderate. **Readiness:** comfortable spending nights away with classmates. |
| 5 | INTERNATIONAL PROGRAMME | **Age:** older students, [VERIFY: TMC's usual grade range]. **Experience:** a different culture, language and way of doing things. **Time:** the longest trip, plus passport and visa lead time. **Family budget:** the highest. **Readiness:** settled with time away, unfamiliar food and routines, and travel documents. |
| 6 | HOW TO CHOOSE | 1. Match the purpose. 2. Check time and student readiness. 3. Check what families can afford. If the purpose can be met closer to home, that is a good choice, not a step down. |
| 7 CTA | PLANNING NEXT YEAR? | Set purpose, grade, dates and budget before you ask for quotes. Programme brief template: link in the post. |

**Post copy:**

Day trip, domestic tour or international programme?

The right answer depends less on the destination and more on five things: student age, the experience you want, the time you have, what families can afford and how ready students are to be away from home.

Start with purpose. Then choose the format.

And if a closer, shorter programme meets the purpose, that is a good decision, not a compromise.
