## A1. Checklist infographic (school buyers)

**LinkedIn:** a single image 1080 × 1350, or a 3-panel document post. **Instagram:** 1080 × 1350.

**Title (Staatliches):** BEFORE PARENTS PAY

**Subtitle:** Seven cost and booking questions your school should settle

| # | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is the total payable cost per student, including taxes? | The figure parents see should be the figure they pay. |
| 2 | What is not included? | Exclusions become extra collections later. |
| 3 | When are payments due, and in how many instalments? | Families need dates they can plan around. |
| 4 | What is the minimum group size, and what happens to the price if fewer students join? | Many per-student prices assume a set number of students. |
| 5 | Under what conditions can the price change after booking? | Fuel, exchange rates and airfares can move a quoted price. |
| 6 | What are the cancellation charges, and from which date do they apply? | Charges often rise as the departure date gets closer. |
| 7 | What is refunded, when and how, if a student withdraws or the trip is cancelled? | Parents will ask. Your school should know the answer first. |

**Footer:** Terms differ by programme and provider. Get them in writing for your trip.

**LinkedIn post copy:**

Before parents pay for a school trip, seven questions are worth settling with the provider.

Total cost including taxes. Exclusions. Payment dates. Minimum group size. Price-change conditions. Cancellation charges. Refunds.

None of these is complicated. All of them are easier to settle before the first payment than after it.

Terms differ by programme, so ask for them in writing for your trip.
