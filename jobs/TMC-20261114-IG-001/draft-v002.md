## Working notes

- Reader: school decision-makers for A1. Parents for A2, sent by the school.
- Job: list the seven cost and booking questions a school settles before parents pay.
- Evidence: these assets state what to ask. They state no TMC terms. Any version quoting TMC's own terms needs operations to verify them per programme (CLM-011).
- Angle: the questions parents ask after payment, answered by the school before it.
- Weakest point: a checklist is easy for any operator to copy. A version showing TMC's own answers would be stronger once operations confirms the terms.

## A1. Checklist infographic (school buyers)

LinkedIn: a single image 1080 × 1350, or a 3-panel document post. Instagram: 1080 × 1350.

**Title (Staatliches):** Before parents pay

**Subtitle:** Seven cost and booking questions to settle with your provider

| # | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is the total payable cost per student, including taxes? | The figure parents see should be the figure they pay. |
| 2 | What is not included? | Exclusions turn into extra collections later. |
| 3 | When are payments due, and in how many instalments? | Families need dates to plan around. |
| 4 | What is the minimum group size, and what happens to the price if fewer students join? | Many per-student prices assume a set number of students. |
| 5 | Under what conditions does the price change after booking? | Fuel costs, exchange rates and airfares sometimes move a quoted price. |
| 6 | What are the cancellation charges, and from which date do they apply? | Charges often rise as the departure date gets closer. |
| 7 | What is refunded, when and how, if a student withdraws or the trip is cancelled? | Parents will ask. Your school should know the answer first. |

**Footer:** Terms differ by programme and provider. Get them in writing for your trip.

**LinkedIn post copy:**

Before parents pay for a school trip, settle seven questions with the provider. They cover total cost including taxes, exclusions, payment dates, minimum group size, price-change conditions, cancellation charges and refunds.

None of these is complicated, and all of them are easier to settle before the first payment than after it. Terms differ by programme, so ask for them in writing for your trip.
