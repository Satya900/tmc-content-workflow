## A1. Blog (listicle)

**Title:** Five budget decisions to review before reducing your school programme

**Slug (proposal):** `/blog/school-trip-budget-decisions`

**Meta description (proposal):** A fixed budget for a school trip? Five decisions to review, in order, before you cut the activities that make the trip worth taking.

---

The budget is fixed and the quotation is over it. The quickest cut is usually the activity list. It is also the cut that most often removes the reason for going.

Before you reduce the programme, review these five decisions. Each one changes the cost. Each one changes something for students too. The aim is to save money where it costs the least learning.

> One line does not move: agreed safety requirements are not a saving option. Every change below keeps them in place.

### 1. Dates

Prices for transport and accommodation can vary with the season, the day of the week and local holidays. Moving the trip by a few weeks, or from a weekend to weekdays, can lower the cost without changing anything students do.

- **What changes for students:** check the exam calendar and the weather at the new dates.

### 2. Transport

The mode, vehicle size and schedule all affect cost. An overnight train instead of a flight, or a vehicle sized to the real group number, can make a large difference.

- **Effect in the worked example:** [NEEDS INPUT]
- **What changes for students:** journey time and comfort. A long journey also takes time away from the programme.

### 3. Duration

One night fewer removes a night of accommodation, meals and staffing. It also removes a day of programme.

- **Effect in the worked example:** [NEEDS INPUT]
- **What changes for students:** less time at each place. Check which activities you would lose and whether the purpose still holds.

### 4. Distance

A destination closer to Bengaluru can meet the same learning purpose for less. A heritage study, a science visit or a nature programme often has a strong option within a shorter journey.

- **Effect in the worked example:** [NEEDS INPUT]
- **What changes for students:** a different place. Check that it still serves the purpose, not just the budget.

### 5. Activities

Review activities last. Keep every activity that serves the learning purpose. Remove the ones that are only there to fill time.

- **Effect in the worked example:** [NEEDS INPUT]
- **What changes for students:** this is the change they notice most. Cut carefully.

### Why this order

Dates and transport usually change the least for students. Duration and distance change the trip itself. Activities are often why you are going. Work down the list, and stop when you are within budget. [VERIFY: Yasin to confirm this order as TMC guidance]

### If none of the five is enough

A different programme type may fit better. A strong day programme can serve a purpose better than a stretched tour. Schools move between day, domestic and international programmes in both directions, and stepping down is a sound choice when it protects the purpose.
