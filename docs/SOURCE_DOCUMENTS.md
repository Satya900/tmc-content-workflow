## Precedence
1. The approval brief governs positioning, audience and content buckets. It is the most recent TMC-specific document.
2. The Understanding TMC guide supplies persona detail, programmes and metrics where the brief is silent.
3. The workbook supplies methods and templates. It is generic course material, so its examples, platform statistics and B2C tactics are not TMC facts.
4. `brand-brief.md` governs visual identity and caption rules. Existing evidence, consent and approval rules in `system/WORKFLOW.md` are unchanged.

## Decisions made while adapting
- **Customer focus is B2B.** The workbook asks "B2C, B2B or B2Ch?". TMC is B2B: the school is the customer. Parents and students are voices and evidence, never sales targets. Only the workbook's B2B channel tactics are used. Lines in the Understanding TMC guide that name parents or students as lead sources are read as B2B (table in `content-strategy-workbook.md`, section 7).
- **Workbook exercises answered.** Every exercise in the workbook is answered for TMC as proposed working answers: customer focus; B2B account and buying-committee profiling; psychographics; five personas; the five bucket research questions and top 10 keywords; needs and emotions for all seven buckets (the workbook asks for two); three full 33-angle topic sets (the workbook asks for one); 15 blog titles; interview guests; school testimonial questions; lead magnets per bucket; platform choice; channel roles; the owner's role; a B2B calendar sample.
- **Buckets replace themes.** The seven buckets replace the earlier seven marketing themes. A mapping table for older jobs is in `content-buckets.md`.
- **Audience roles follow the brief.** Owners, trustees, management and principals decide. Academic leaders and teachers influence. Parents and students are voices. The guide's primary, secondary and tertiary personas are kept as profile detail.
- **Six emotions.** The Decode step uses the workbook's six categories. "Aspiration" in older files maps to Fantasies.
- **USPs are claims.** The guide's selling points (safety protocols, track record, testimonials, customisation, holistic development) are seeded as `VERIFY` claims, not facts.
- **Internal data stays internal.** Targets, school segments and the product funnel are marked `PROHIBITED` for publication. School segments use neutral labels A to D in place of the guide's names.
- **"Quantity first, quality second"** is read as "publish consistently". It never overrides fact, consent or review rules.
- **Video scripts in scope, production out.** `tmc-reel` now covers Reels, YouTube, interview, promo and testimonial scripts. Video production stays deferred.
- **Two new skills.** `tmc-social-post` (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, Google Business Profile, X, infographics) and `tmc-lead-magnet` (checklists, guides, assessments, landing pages).
- **Paid ads and account setup are excluded.** The workbook's ad-account and platform setup steps are not part of the content pipeline.

## Open questions for the team
1. **Bucket approval.** The brief asks for approval or amendments "on page 3". The supplied file has one page of content and no page 3. Record the team's decision here when it is made.
2. **Operating standards.** The brief says agreement is needed on "operating standards". They are not in the supplied file. Until supplied, the standards in `system/WORKFLOW.md` apply.
3. **Posting cadence.** The contracted capacity is 25 non-video placements a month. The guide's ideal schedule is about 74 plus video, and includes X (16 a month). Which number should calendars plan to, and is X in scope?
4. **Channel-specific GPT templates.** The brief mentions building them after bucket approval. The format skills in `skills/` are the current templates for Claude and ChatGPT. Confirm whether separate per-channel files are wanted.
5. **Shared calendar.** The guide links a Google Sheet sample calendar. It is not connected. Confirm whether the calendar columns in `measurement-and-calendar.md` should match that sheet exactly.
6. **Claims evidence.** Which of CLM-001 to CLM-007 can be approved, and with what wording?
7. **Workbook answers.** The answers in `content-strategy-workbook.md` are proposed from the source documents. Confirm or correct them, especially the buying-committee roles, the needs and emotions tables and the top 10 keywords (check against search data).
8. **Curriculum boards.** Which boards does TMC serve (CBSE, ICSE, IB, Cambridge, state)? Until confirmed, content must not claim board alignment.
9. **Who pays.** When parents fund a trip through the school, the school is still the customer in this system. Confirm the team agrees.
