TMC Content Platform

Development proposal and operating cost estimate

Prepared for The Modern Classroom<br/>27 September 2026 | Version 1

This proposal covers a dedicated TMC web application for planning, drafting, reviewing and exporting non-video content. The fixed development fee is <b>₹2,00,000</b>, with delivery targeted within <b>one month</b> of scope approval and receipt of required inputs.

TMC should budget <b>₹6,000–₹10,000 per month</b> for the core platform services. Optional publishing subscriptions and ongoing developer maintenance are separate. Human approval remains required before content is published.

Cost category | Proposed budget

One-time development | ₹2,00,000

Core monthly operating budget | ₹6,000–₹10,000

Core annual operating budget | ₹72,000–₹1,20,000

First year including development | ₹2,72,000–₹3,20,000

All amounts exclude applicable taxes, foreign exchange fees and optional services. Annual figures assume 12 full months of operation. Operating budgets are estimates, not fixed-price or unlimited-use commitments.

What the investment delivers

One workspace for TMC knowledge, content briefs, generated drafts, branded post templates, review history, exports and a content calendar. The existing TMC knowledge and skill materials will inform the application workflow.

Planning basis

One brand, 2–5 application users and approximately 25 non-video content placements per month, with moderate revisions. The build uses existing approved photographs and a limited template library. Video production and Ready for Umrah are outside this proposal.

<b>Commercial status:</b> The development fee and one-month target reflect the current discussion. Scope quantities, milestone terms and support provisions below are proposed for agreement before kickoff. This web application proposal does not automatically amend or add to the earlier desktop-workflow agreement.

Development scope and fee allocation




The following allocation explains the fixed ₹2,00,000 development fee. It is a scope breakdown, not an hourly rate card or a menu of independently priced modules.

Workstream | Included work | Allocation

Requirements and design | Workflow specification, screen design and data model | ₹20,000

Application foundation | Login, roles, TMC knowledge and media records | ₹35,000

AI content workflow | Briefs, format prompts, draft generation, edits and versions | ₹40,000

Visual templates and exports | Three template families with PNG and PDF exports | ₹35,000

Review and job management | Approval states, calendar, job retries and notifications | ₹30,000

Testing and handover | Acceptance checks, deployment, training and documentation | ₹40,000

Total fixed development fee |  | ₹2,00,000

Included content formats

Founder and company LinkedIn posts, LinkedIn PDF carousels, Instagram static posts and carousels, Facebook adaptations, newsletter copy, blog drafts and website copy. A calendar entry is a planned placement. Drafting these formats does not include a monthly managed content service.

Proposed template and editing limits

Three visual template families: educational carousel, tour photo carousel and static announcement. Include square and portrait image variants where appropriate and a PDF carousel export. Users can edit text, select photographs and reorder slides through form controls. A freeform Canva-style design editor is excluded.

Proposed workflow

Brief and sources → draft generation → editorial checks → human review → approved version → branded export → calendar and publication record. Editing an approved draft requires fresh approval. Media records capture permission status, and unknown permission blocks use of identifiable student media.

The base scope produces approved publishing assets. Direct social publishing, newsletter sending, CMS publishing and automated analytics ingestion require separately confirmed integrations.

What does not require another subscription

Next.js, TypeScript and the HTML/CSS rendering code carry no software subscription charge. Playwright rendering uses the background compute budget above. GitHub Free is suitable for the initial code repository within its allowances [6]. Database and media storage start within Supabase allowances.

What can increase the bill

Long source documents, repeated draft regeneration, more users or paid developer seats, additional database projects, accumulated photographs, large downloads and render jobs can increase usage. Generated images, research APIs and marketing email delivery are outside the base estimate.

Production accounts should be owned and paid for by TMC. Configure usage alerts and available spending controls, then review actual consumption after the first month. Budget alerts are not a guarantee that every provider will stop charging at a fixed amount.

Operating cost ownership

TMC pays vendor subscriptions and usage directly. The development fee covers the agreed implementation work, not recurring vendor bills. The developer’s normal coding tools and internal productivity subscriptions are part of the developer’s delivery overhead and are not separate client subscriptions under this proposal.

Recommended starting configuration

Launch with Vercel, Supabase, one AI API provider, Trigger.dev and the custom renderer. Use the notification service within its initial allowance. Add publishing only after the team has accepted the content and export workflow and the required formats have passed a live integration test.

Delivery milestones and acceptance




The one-month target starts after scope approval, the kickoff payment and access to required accounts, brand assets and source material. A nominated TMC reviewer should provide consolidated feedback within two working days. External approvals and delayed inputs may move the delivery date.

Period | Delivery milestone

Week 1 | Confirm scope and templates. Build login, roles, knowledge records, media intake and the basic dashboard.

Week 2 | Implement format workflows, AI generation, editorial checks, editing, version history and review states.

Week 3 | Implement the three template families, PNG/PDF exports, job retries, notifications and calendar.

Week 4 | Run client acceptance checks, correct agreed defects, deploy, train the team and hand over source and documentation.

Proposed payment schedule

Milestone | Share | Amount

Scope approval and kickoff | 40% | ₹80,000

Working pilot accepted | 30% | ₹60,000

Final acceptance and handover | 30% | ₹60,000

Total | 100% | ₹2,00,000

Acceptance checks

The pilot must demonstrate a brief becoming an editable draft, a saved revision, a reviewer decision and an approved export. Final acceptance should cover at least one example of every included content format and each of the three visual template families.

Also verify role access, rejection and revision handling, approval invalidation after edits, media permission blocking, failed-job recovery and readable PNG/PDF exports. AI checks assist the reviewer and do not certify that every generated statement is accurate.

Handover

Provide the application source, database structure, deployment instructions, account access handover, prompt and template configuration, and a team operating guide. Include one training session and an agreed backup and recovery procedure. TMC supplies a reviewer to complete acceptance.

Commercial assumptions and references




Proposed support and change terms

Include 30 calendar days of correction for reproducible defects against the accepted scope after launch. New features, additional templates, content operations and third-party API changes are separate work. An ongoing maintenance retainer, response times and service hours require a separate agreement. These are proposed terms for confirmation, not previously accepted commitments.

TMC supplies approved brand assets, factual source material, media permissions, account access and review decisions. Requested additions receive a written scope, price and timeline before work begins. No growth, engagement, lead-generation or time-saving outcome is guaranteed.

Items outside the fixed fee

Video and avatar workflows, Ready for Umrah, multiple brands, a freeform design editor, unlimited template revisions, bulk historical migration, paid advertising, direct CMS publishing, automated social analytics, research subscriptions and ongoing monthly content production. Social publishing and marketing email integrations are also excluded unless added in writing.

Approval before kickoff

Confirm the included formats and three template families, nominated reviewer, production account ownership, operating budget and payment/support terms. Confirm in writing how this proposal relates to any earlier ₹80,000 workflow agreement so neither party assumes an unagreed credit or additional charge. Applicable development taxes will be stated on the formal quotation or invoice.
