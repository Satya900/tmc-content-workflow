1. White TMC planning slide with cyan accents. Text: Before choosing a destination. Three questions for your next school-trip planning meeting.  Start with the students and the purpose of the trip.

2. White TMC planning slide with cyan accents. Text: Give the shortlist a purpose. A place can be interesting and still need a closer look for this group. What will students explore? What pace can the group manage? What do families need to know? 

3. White TMC planning slide with cyan accents. Text: What should students explore in person?. Name a question students can investigate through observation. History: how does a place remember its past? Geography: how does a city manage water? Plan time to discuss what students notice.

4. White TMC planning slide with cyan accents. Text: What is manageable for this group?. Consider age, travel experience and accessibility needs. Count transfers and walking time. Allow for meals and rest. Discuss time away from home. Review the full day, not only the main visit.

5. White TMC planning slide with cyan accents. Text: What do families need to understand?. Prepare clear information before asking families to commit. Expected cost and what it includes. Supervision and communication arrangements. What is confirmed and what needs checking. 

6. White TMC planning slide with cyan accents. Text: Write the brief before the shortlist. A useful brief connects the learning purpose, the needs of the group and the questions families will ask.  Use it as the starting point for comparing destinations.
