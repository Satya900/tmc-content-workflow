# TMC Core Context

## Purpose
Keep every TMC output aligned with the same positioning, audience logic, content buckets, voice, content categories and factual boundaries.

## Before doing any TMC work
0. Read the repository's `brand-brief.md` for visual identity and caption rules. In a portable desktop pack, use the bundled TMC_CONTEXT.md instead. Read `system/WORKFLOW.md` for the current operating scope when working in this repository.
1. Read `references/tmc-brand-context.md` (positioning, objective, audience roles, programmes, SEL values, voice).
2. Read `references/content-buckets.md` and assign one primary bucket (five-bucket structure). If the bucket has a review reference (bucket 1: `references/bucket-1-programme-choice-price-value.md`), read it, use its topic IDs, locked Decode categories, item specs and production guide, and check its selection register before producing final content.
3. For content creation or planning, read `references/audience-personas.md`, `references/marketing-template-framework.md` (the 4D process and 33 angles) and `references/content-strategy-workbook.md` (the answered workbook: buying committee, bucket research, channel roles). `references/topic-bank.md` holds idea material. Both use the superseded seven-bucket numbering: map with `content-buckets.md`, and a bucket's review reference always takes precedence.
4. Before writing any copy, read `references/writing-standards.md`: style, format, restricted words, the reasoning framework and the self-check. It applies to every format.
5. For a specific format or channel, read the relevant section of `references/content-generation-playbook.md`.
6. For a marketing asset, apply `references/content-taxonomy.md`.
7. For factual claims, use the claims register if available and follow `references/claims-register-template.md`.
8. For calendars, cadence or performance, read `references/measurement-and-calendar.md`.

## Non-negotiables
- TMC is **B2B-led**: the school is the customer and school decision-makers come first. Parents and guardians are a secondary audience because they fund participation, give consent and judge affordability. They are reached through school routes (material supplied to the school, school-led sharing) and parent-friendly carousels, and parent material directs questions to the school coordinator. Students are a tertiary audience, reached only through school and parent routes. Parents and students are never collected as sales leads.
- Produce final content only for items selected in a bucket's selection register. Unselected items get a labelled pre-selection sample only on explicit request.
- Quality and relevance before quantity. No monthly quota is fixed.
- Distinguish TOUR content from MARKETING content before writing.
- Position TMC as an educational experience design partner, never a generic travel operator.
- One primary audience and one primary content bucket per asset.
- Do not invent TMC facts, school facts, student outcomes, testimonials, prices, safety claims, founder anecdotes or permissions.
- TMC's selling points in its own planning documents are claims to verify, not facts.
- Internal planning data (targets, school segments, product funnel labels) is never published.
- Prefer practical educational value over generic inspiration.
- Human review is required before publication.
- Every draft follows `references/writing-standards.md` and passes its self-check before REVIEW. Be direct with the team: name weak briefs, gaps and unsupported claims instead of agreeing to be polite.
