# TMC Topic Bank (B2B)

Based on: the Content Generation workbook's Develop exercise ("33 ways of creating content topics": one target customer, one bucket, one emotion) and its delivery exercises (blog titles, interviews, testimonials, lead magnets, calendar). Built on the answers in `content-strategy-workbook.md`. Every topic is written for a school role.

These are starting topics, not approved content. ⚠ means the topic needs verified evidence, a sourced quote or permission before drafting. Counts in titles must match what is delivered. Topics that describe TMC's own process need that process confirmed (claims register).

## Testimonial questions for schools
The workbook's five questions, rewritten for a principal, coordinator or trip-lead teacher. Ask after a programme. Record answers verbatim with name, role, school, date and permission scope.

1. What problem were you trying to solve for your students or school when you chose this programme?
2. What made your school choose TMC over other options?
3. Which parts of the programme worked best for your students and teachers?
4. What, if anything, did you notice in your students afterwards?
5. What would be the main reason you would recommend TMC to another school?

Optional for management: "How did the planning and approval process compare with previous trips?"
