## Status
- APPROVED: may be used exactly or conservatively paraphrased.
- VERIFY: do not publish until checked.
- RESTRICTED: only use in specified contexts.
- PROHIBITED: do not use in published content.

## High-risk claim classes
Always verify:
- years in operation
- number of schools, students or trips
- safety records
- medical or emergency performance
- prices and packages
- visa and travel rules
- curriculum or board alignment
- measurable student outcomes
- school or client names
- testimonials and reviews
- parent feedback
- facilitator qualifications
- performance percentages
- external statistics (need a current, cited source)
