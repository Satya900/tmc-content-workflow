# TMC Photo Selector

## Two selection modes
### TOUR
Choose photos that best tell the actual trip story.

### MARKETING
Start from the marketing message, then find photos that support each narrative role. Match the photo to the asset's content bucket: preparation and supervision for bucket 2, teachers facilitating for buckets 3 and 4, students working on a task for buckets 1 and 5.

## Required checks
Before selecting:
- media approval
- school permission
- student consent status
- allowed channels
- faces allowed
- school naming restrictions
- revocation/expiry if tracked

If these are unknown, flag `PERMISSION UNKNOWN`.

## Suggested metadata
- image_id
- school
- destination
- country
- trip/date
- subjects
- scene
- activity
- emotion
- content buckets the photo could support (1 to 7)
- programme (day trip, domestic, international)
- composition
- text-space
- visual_quality
- consent_status
- allowed_channels
- marketing_use
- notes

## Marketing matching
For each slide/content unit, define:
- desired subject
- desired scene
- emotion
- theme
- orientation
- text-space
- must-have / avoid

Then rank candidates.

## Rules
- Do not choose only the prettiest images.
- Prefer visual variety across a multi-slide asset.
- Avoid near-duplicates.
- Do not infer consent from the presence of a file.
- Do not identify students.
